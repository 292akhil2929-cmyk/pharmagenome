# CellMiner NCI-60 source notice

This curated snapshot is adapted from the National Cancer Institute CellMiner 2025.3 processed downloads: DTP NCI-60 average drug-activity z scores, protein-function-affecting exome variants, and five-platform gene-expression z scores.

Source: https://discover.nci.nih.gov/cellminer/loadDownload.do

NCI states that its text is generally free of copyright unless otherwise indicated and asks reusers to credit the National Cancer Institute. This repository credits NCI and does not use the NCI logo. Source data and third-party material remain subject to their original terms.

Citations requested by CellMiner: Shankavaram et al., BMC Genomics (2009); Reinhold et al., Cancer Research (2012). The curated file preserves the predeclared PharmaGenome ten-gene panel, ten named FDA-approved drug records, and their aligned NCI-60 cell-line values.

These are research data. They do not establish clinical response, efficacy, safety, or treatment suitability.
